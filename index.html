🔌 Connect your AI (needed once)

Hit "Test connection" first if things aren't working — it checks reachability and, for a local model, tells you exactly which models Ollama actually has pulled. This calls whichever API you configure, directly from your browser — the transcript leaves your device only when you tap "Generate". Output now streams in as it's generated rather than appearing all at once. On a local CPU-only model, generation itself is still the slow part (no GPU acceleration) — the biggest speed win is a smaller model: try ollama pull llama3.2:3b or ollama pull qwen2.5:3b (both a fraction of llama3.1's size, noticeably faster, still fine quality for this task) and set the Model field to match. OpenRouter currently offers DeepSeek's chat model free of charge (model IDs change occasionally — check openrouter.ai/models if the default stops working) and, since it runs on real GPUs, responds in a few seconds regardless of your own hardware.

Your result will appear here after you generate it.
The overview and suggestions are decision-support only — it does not diagnose students, and it is not a substitute for professional judgment. If a transcript contains any indication of risk to a student's safety, escalate through your school's normal safeguarding process regardless of what this tool says.
Speech recognition runs in your browser (works best in Chrome or Edge) and requires microphone access. Translation auto-detects the language of each sentence and moves through a few free services if one is unreachable or rate-limited, so accuracy on slang or heavy code-switching may still vary — good enough for notes, not for anything legal or medical. Your transcript is saved automatically in this browser as you go, so refreshing the page or closing the tab won't lose it — it'll still be here next time you open this file, until you press "Clear conversation." (It's stored only on this device/browser, not shared anywhere else.)
